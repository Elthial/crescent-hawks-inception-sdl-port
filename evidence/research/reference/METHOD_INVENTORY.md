## Meaning of ASM confirmed

Sol: Reset on 2026-09-17. A blank **ASM confirmed** cell means no fresh systematic audit has been recorded. Historical review links are kept separately and NEVER imply confirmation. Fresh statuses come only from explicit `ASM_AUDIT_RECORDS.json` records tied to the current method-body SHA-256. Changed bodies become STALE until checked again.

**Fully matched** means all meaningful local operations and paths were accounted for against ASM, with named intentional substitutions/omissions documented. It is not literal line correspondence, a claim that this pseudo-C compiles, or certification of called routines. **Checked - mismatch** means a complete comparison found discrepancies that remain in C. **Partial** identifies incomplete coverage. Port disposition is independent: replaced DOS routines and added research helpers are not marked confirmed merely because they will not be copied into the port.

This is an index of all retained definitions plus every additional routine entry recognised by the clean per-segment Reko ASM headers. An ASM entry can be a compiler helper, internal entry or adapter variant, not necessarily a distinct original C function. Unknown and omitted routines remain explicit rather than being invented. Data-only segment files contain no method definitions.
